# Specials

This document defines every **Special** that appears on Breakaway cards — what it does, when it triggers, and how it interacts with the game. Specials are unique effects that fall outside of standard Actions and Abilities.

---

| Special | Card | Summary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Breakaway** | Breakaway | Grants the Purple (Doubles) action for free, twice |

## Detailed Descriptions

### Breakaway
**Card:** Breakaway

The Breakaway special is triggered when the Breakaway card is played. It grants the player the **Purple ability (Doubles)** for **free, twice** during that play sequence. The Doubles ability doubles the power number of the next card played in a challenge. Using it twice effectively allows the player to apply the bonus across two consecutive plays without spending an ability token.

---

### Timeout
**Card:** Timeout

When played, Timeout **pauses the current turn** before any card action resolves. The player who played Timeout may then perform **up to 2 free bench swaps** — exchanging cards between their hand and bench — before nomally resuming play. After swaps are made, the turn continues as if the Timeout had not occurred.

- ⚠ **Restriction Level I** — only 1 Timeout card is allowed per deck.

---

### You Missed
**Card:** Miss

When played, **You Missed removes both itself and the last card played from play** — as if that previous card had never been played. All actions, abilities, and specials that card triggered are fully reverted:

- Any puck movement caused by that card is undone
- Any challenge (Shot, Body-Check, etc.) it initiated is cancelled
- Any ability or special it activated is nullified
- Both cards go to their respective owner's discard piles

The Miss card can be played **at any point** immediately after the opponent (or the player themselves) resolves or begins resolving a card. It cannot revert a card that was played more than one action ago.

> **Example:** The opponent plays "Shoot the Puck" and initiates a Shot on Goal. Before the challenge resolves, the other player plays "Miss" — the Shoot action is cancelled, both cards are discarded, and the game state returns to before the shot was taken.

---

### Dig it Out
**Card:** Superstar

When a **stoppage** occurs — most commonly when a goalie plays a **Save** action — play would normally halt and reset with a faceoff. **Dig it Out** allows the attacking player to immediately respond by playing their **Superstar** card to override the stoppage and **force play to continue**.

**How it works:**
1. A stoppage is triggered (e.g. a Save is made, or any other play-stopping effect resolves).
2. Before the faceoff or reset takes place, the attacking player may play their **Superstar** card from hand.
3. Activating the **Dig it Out** special cancels the stoppage — play resumes from the puck's current position as if nothing stopped it.
4. The Superstar card is then discarded to its owner's discard pile as normal.

> **Example:** The opponent's goalie plays a Save card, stopping your shot. You have a Superstar card in hand. You immediately play it and activate Dig it Out — the save is overridden, the stoppage is cancelled, and play continues with the puck still in the offensive zone.

- ⚠ Dig it Out can only be played **immediately** after a stoppage is declared — it cannot be used retroactively or on a delayed basis.
- The Superstar card's other attributes (number, suits, abilities) are still available alongside the special if the player chooses to activate them.

---

### Penalty Kill
**Card:** Penalty Kill

The **Penalty Kill** special is only active when the player's team is currently serving a penalty (i.e., playing short-handed on an active penalty kill). While the penalty kill is in effect, playing this card grants **free access to the colour ability of the K symbol** displayed on the card — not the card's own background colour.

- The K symbol on the Penalty Kill card has its own designated colour *(to be defined)*. That colour's ability is what is granted for free.
- Outside of an active penalty kill, the card plays as a normal number card with no special bonus.
- This represents the short-handed unit rallying to defend and counter-attack under pressure.

> **Note:** The specific colour and ability of the K symbol will be defined during card design finalisation.

---

### Penalty
**Card:** Elbow

The **Penalty** special turns the Elbow card into a reactive trap. While the Elbow card remains in the play area, any player who plays a card **containing the same colour as the P symbol** displayed on the Elbow card immediately receives a **2-minute minor penalty** — regardless of whose turn it is.

- The penalised player must sit out for **2 turns**, playing short-handed until the penalty expires or the opponent scores.
- The P symbol has its own designated colour *(to be defined)*. Only cards sharing that specific colour trigger the penalty.
- A player who does not have any cards matching the P colour is safe to play freely.
- The Elbow card remains in the play area as an active trap until the turn ends or it is removed by another effect.

> **Note:** The specific colour of the P symbol will be defined during card design finalisation.

---


### Crowd
**Card:** Crowd

When the Crowd card is played, the player may **choose any 1 action or ability** from the card most recently played by either player and apply it as if it were on the Crowd card itself. This represents the momentum of the home crowd fuelling an unexpected burst of energy. The chosen effect resolves immediately.

- 🥈 The Crowd card's **Silver ability** allows the player to choose an action OR ability — the Crowd special additionally allows them to copy from the **opponent's** last played card as well.

---

### U — Utility Marker
**Card:** All Utility cards (Faceoff, Finesse, Elite, Utility)

The **U** marker identifies a card as a **Utility card**, making it eligible to trigger Promotion Specials. Utility cards are wild in terms of suits — they count as all 4 suits for deck-building purposes — but their Promotion Special is what gives them strategic power.

## Promotion Specials

Promotion Specials are a unique category of special that can **only be activated by playing a Utility card on top of a Player Card**. Player Cards are numbered **1 through 7** (Rookie, Specialist, Enforcer, Sniper, Winger, Centre, Defenceman).

When a Promotion Special is activated:
- The Utility card is discarded to the player's discard pile.
- The **target Player Card** permanently gains the ability granted by the promotion for the rest of the game.
- This is tracked visually on the **player's number grid hidden tab** — the number corresponding to that Player Card is highlighted with the promotion's colour overlay.

> **Example:** Playing Elite Utility on a Winger (number 5) grants the Winger the 🔵 Blue ability. In the position grid tab, the number **5** is shown with a blue colour overlay.

---

### Promotion Special: Face-off
**Card:** Faceoff Utility

When played on a Player Card (1–7), grants that card the **🥉 Bronze ability**.

- Bronze: Always wins Face-offs against non-Bronze cards; ties are broken by the higher number.
- The promoted Player Card's number is shown with a **bronze colour overlay** in the position grid tab.

---

### Promotion Special: Finesse
**Card:** Finesse Utility

When played on a Player Card (1–7), grants that card the **🟠 Orange ability**.

- Orange: Grants a free Finesse action — the player may move the puck one area toward the opponent's net without spending a Move card.
- The promoted Player Card's number is shown with an **orange colour overlay** in the position grid tab.

---

### Promotion Special: Elite
**Card:** Elite Utility

When played on a Player Card (1–7), grants that card the **🔵 Blue ability**.

- Blue: Grants the **Move** action (advance puck one area), **On-Net** (starts the On a Shot phase), and **Score** (score a goal on a shot card).
- The promoted Player Card's number is shown with a **blue colour overlay** in the position grid tab.

---

### Promotion Special: Utility
**Card:** Utility

When played on a Player Card (1–7), grants that card the **🟤 Brown ability**.

- Brown: Grants the Body-Check action — allowing this player card to initiate a physical challenge to steal possession.
- The promoted Player Card's number is shown with a **brown colour overlay** in the position grid tab.

---

## Notes

- **Specials are optional** — a player may choose not to activate a special when playing a card.
- **Promotion Specials** can only be activated by playing a Utility card on top of a **Player Card (1–7)** — they cannot target non-player cards and cannot be copied by the Crowd or Silver abilities.
- **Restriction levels** (I, II) on some cards (e.g. Timeout) limit how many copies may be included in a single deck.
- The **P symbol colour** and **K symbol colour** on the Elbow and Penalty Kill cards are to be defined during card design finalisation.
