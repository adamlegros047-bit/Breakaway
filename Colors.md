# Ability Colors

Each card in **Breakaway** may carry a colored ability that grants a special bonus when that card wins a challenge or is resolved as a perk. The player who selects an ability during Card Resolution receives its effect.

---

**Effect:** Allows the player to initiate or participate in a Grind challenge.
