## Special Notes

- **Goalie**: Has special handling for Save/Block actions.
- **Utility Cards**: Marked with an 'U' special, used for specific strategic plays.
- **Restriction Levels**: Some cards (like Referee, Linesman, Timeout) have restriction levels (I, II) that affect when they can be played.
- **Body Check**: While the base abilities are Brown, the specific artwork varies by deck (Home vs Away).
