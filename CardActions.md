## Special Notes

- **Goalie**: Has special handling for Save/Block actions.
- **Utility Cards**: Marked with a 'U' special, used for specific strategic plays.
- **Restriction Levels**: Some cards (like Referee, Linesman, Timeout) have restriction levels (I, II) that indicate how many of these cards are allowed in a Deck.
- **Body Check**: Artwork varies by deck (Home vs Away), but the Brown ability is consistent.
- **Drawbacks**: Referee has *Game Misconduct on abuse*; Rookie has *-2 Power vs Veterans*; Enforcer has *2-Min High Sticking Chance*.
- **Breakaway Special**: The Breakaway card carries its own named special, giving the player the Purple action (Doubles) for free twice.
- **You Missed Special**: The Miss card carries the *You Missed* special, triggering its unique miss rules when played.
- **Timeout Special**: The Timeout card carries the *Timeout* special, allowing a player to pause and reset play under specific conditions.
