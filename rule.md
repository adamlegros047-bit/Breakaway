# BREAKAWAY: Official Rulebook

## 1. Game Setup
- **Deck**: The game is played with a 60-card deck.
- **Bench**: Each player starts with 5 specific cards on their bench:
  - 🏒 **Power Play**
  - 🧤 **Goalie**
  - 📋 **Coaching**
  - 🔄 **Line Change**
  - ⏱️ **Timeout**
- **Hand**: Each player is dealt 5 random cards from their shuffled deck.
- **Pre-Faceoff**: Before the first face-off, players may swap up to **two (2)** cards between their hand and bench.

## 2. Match Flow
- **Periods**: A standard game consists of 3 periods.
- **Stoppages**: Game stops after a Goal, Save, Icing, or Penalty.
- **Face-off**: Every period and stoppage restarts with a Face-off.

## 3. Challenges & Resolution
### Face-off
1. Both players select one card for the face-off.
2. Comapre numbers: **Higher number wins** possession.
3. **Suit Tie-Breaker**: If numbers match, the higher suit wins:
   - 🏒 **Stick** (Rank 4)
   - ⛸️ **Skate** (Rank 3)
   - 🧤 **Glove** (Rank 2)
   - ⛑️ **Helmet** (Rank 1)
4. **Re-draw**: If both number and suit match, both players discard and re-draw.

### Puck Battles (Neutral Zone)
- Compare card values. Certain specialty cards (Poke Check, Body Check) provide bonuses.

## 4. Scoring & Goaltending
- **Shot**: An offensive player with possession in the offensive zone can play a "Shot" card (or any card with a 'Shoot' action/number).
- **Save**: The goalie must respond with a card of **higher value** or a dedicated **Goalie** card with the **Save** action.
- **Goal**: If the goalie fails to save, the offensive team scores.

## 5. Line Changes
- **Standard**: Swapping a hand card with a bench card.
- **Prerequisites**: Only allowed when the puck is in the **Neutral** or **Offensive** zone.
- **Limits**:
  - Max **one (1)** swap per turn during active play.
  - Max **two (2)** swaps during a stoppage (Pre-Faceoff).
  - Max **two (2)** swaps at the start of a period.

## 6. Momentum & Tokens
- **PB Circles**: Momentum is tracked in the PB circles on the board.
- **Earning Tokens**: Winning certain challenges or playing specific cards grants momentum tokens.
