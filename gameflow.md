# BREAKAWAY: Game Flow Guide

This guide breaks down the step-by-step process of playing a match in **Breakaway**, from the opening face-off to the final buzzer.

---

## 1. Game Setup & Pre-Match
*   **Decks**: Both players start with a unique 60-card deck (White for Home, Black for Away).
*   **Starting Hand**: Each player is dealt **5 cards**.
*   **The Bench**: 5 essential tactical cards are placed on your bench (Power Play, Goalie, Coaching, Line Change, Timeout).
*   **Lineup Phase**: Before the opening face-off, you can perform up to **2 swaps** between your hand and bench to set your strategy.

---

## 2. The Face-off (Starting Play)
Every period and game stoppage (Goal, Save, Penalty) begins with a Face-off.
1.  **Selection**: Both players choose one card from their hand.
2.  **Comparison**: The numbers on the cards are compared. High number wins.
3.  **Suit Tie-Breaker**: If numbers are equal, the higher suit wins: 
    *   🏒 **Stick** > ⛸️ **Skate** > 🧤 **Glove** > ⛑️ **Helmet**.
4.  **Resolution**: The winner takes **possession** of the puck and the first turn of active play.

---

## 3. Active Play (The Player Turn)
During your turn, you aim to advance the puck and create scoring opportunities.

### A. Playing a Card
You play one card per turn to perform an action.
*   **Movement/Skating**: Advances the puck between zones.
*   **Defense**: Used to steal possession or block shots.
*   **Specialty**: Triggering unique effects like "Poke Check" or "Body Check".

### B. Advancing the Puck
The rink is divided into three zones relative to your team:
1.  **Defensive Zone**: Your end. You want to clear the puck out.
2.  **Neutral Zone**: Center ice. Use this to transition to the attack.
3.  **Offensive Zone**: The opponent's end. This is where you can score.

### C. Passing & The Shot Bonus
While in the **Offensive Zone**, moving the puck or playing "Passing" cards builds up your **Passing Bonus** (tracked on the HUD).
*   You must have at least **1 Passing Bonus** to attempt a **Shot on Goal**.
*   The more passes you make, the harder your shot becomes to save!

---

## 4. Shooting & Scoring
Once you have a Passing Bonus in the Offensive Zone, you can trigger a **Shot**.
1.  **Initiation**: Play a card with a 'Shoot' action or trigger the 'Shot' command if you have the bonus.
2.  **The Challenge**: A "Shot vs Save" challenge begins.
3.  **Goalie Response**: The defending player must play a card to try and **Save**.
    *   **Goal**: If the Shooter's value is higher, they score! The score updates on the Jumbotron.
    *   **Save**: If the Goalie's value is higher or equal (depending on the specific "Save" action), the puck is stopped.
4.  **Result**: Both Goals and Saves result in a **stoppage**, leading back to a Face-off.

---

## 5. Line Changes (Bench Management)
You can swap cards with your bench to adapt to the game state.
*   **When**: Allowed during any turn where the puck is in the **Neutral** or **Offensive** zone.
*   **Stoppage Swaps**: During face-offs or period breaks, you get free swaps (up to 2) to reset your hand.
*   **Goalie Swap**: Crucial for ensuring you have a "Save" capable card in hand when the opponent enters your Defensive Zone.

---

## 6. Momentum
Winning challenges (Face-offs, Puck Battles) or playing specific high-tier cards earns **Momentum Tokens**.
*   **Usage**: Spend tokens to add a **+1 bonus** to your next played card.
*   **Strategy**: Save tokens for critical Face-offs or to guarantee a Goal on a big shot.

---

## 7. Ending a Turn
A turn ends after:
1.  A card is played and its action is resolved.
2.  The active player triggers "End Turn".
*   **Drawing**: You automatically draw back up to your hand size (usually 5) at the end of your turn.
*   **Switching**: Possession stays with the player until they lose a challenge or end their sequence.

---

## 8. Winning the Game
*   Play through **3 Periods**.
*   The team with the **highest score** on the Jumbotron at the end of the 3rd period wins.
*   If tied, the game moves to **Overtime**.
